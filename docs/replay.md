# Replay

Replay plays a match back in 3D, with every unit drawn as the vehicle or person it is. Use it to watch what happened, compare many matches, or see who hit whom.

- [Match replay](match-replay.md): play one match, with figures, tracers, hits and Tacview-style cameras.
- [Many matches at once](many-matches.md): lay hundreds of matches on top of each other.
- [Figures](figures.md): the shapes used for units, and where they come from.
- [Hit analysis](hit-analysis.md): where one vehicle or soldier was hit, and by whom.

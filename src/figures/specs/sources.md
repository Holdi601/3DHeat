# Figure sources

Drawings and photos the figure specs were measured on or digitised from. Only
public-domain, CC0 and CC-BY material is used for outlines (CC-BY items are also
credited in docs/figure-credits.md); published dimensions (length, beam,
span...) come from reputable references and are not listed here. The images
themselves are never stored in this repository.
